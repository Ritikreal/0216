To Prapthi, my love,

My Prapthi, my favorite person,

I still think about that morning on the hill , taking you there and telling you how I felt around six, while the sun was rising. It was such a big moment for me, and I’ll always be glad that I got to share it with you. But somehow, one of the memories I hold closest is what came after: that cold, quiet walk all the way till Tata Promount. No crowd, no noise, no need to make it into anything grand. It was just you and me, walking and talking in the cold, and I felt so happy simply being beside you. That was the best part.

I think about your birthday, too. It made me feel that this is a day I want to celebrate every year, because it’s about you , the person who means so much to me. I want you to feel loved, remembered, and celebrated, my Munchkin, not just on one date but in all the little ways I can.

And then there’s our little Bannana Bob, our Shiba Inu baby. I love that we imagine taking care of him as our own child, planning a life where we live far out in the woods, with a quiet home and the three of us. No big crowd, no noise, no one else’s expectations , just you and me and our little family, building our own kind of peace. I know it’s a dream we’ve talked about, but I love that it’s a future we can picture together.

These memories and plans are what make “us” feel real to me. I don’t expect every day to be perfect, and I know life won’t always go the way we planned. I just hope we keep talking, keep listening, keep being gentle with each other, and keep making room for more memories like these.

November 24 is our day, but I want to celebrate you far beyond one date. Thank you for being part of my life, Prapthi. If I could go back to that hill, that sunrise, and that quiet walk, I would still want to find my way to you. And for the future , I want us to face the hard things side by side. Not me versus you. Us versus the problem, us versus the world, always choosing to work through it together.

Yours, with all my heart. ♡
Prapthi, please read this when you’re ready

I know I hurt your trust.

Prapthi, I need to be honest about something without trying to make it sound smaller than it was. You gave me your trust, and I misused it. I hurt you and damaged something that should have been safe between us. I know that saying “I’m sorry” doesn’t undo what happened, and I don’t expect a letter to fix the pain or make you trust me again overnight.

What stays with me is that even after I hurt you, you still chose to stay and give us a chance. I know that choice wasn’t something I was entitled to. Your patience and the love you’ve shown me are not things I should take for granted, and I’m sorry that my actions put you in a position where you had to wonder whether your trust in me was safe.

I don’t want to use how much I love you as an excuse for what I did. Love should show up in my choices, in my honesty, in the way I respect your boundaries, and in whether my actions match my words. You deserve consistency, not just promises made when I’m scared of losing you. I need to take responsibility and do the work to become someone whose actions make it easier to feel safe with him again.

I understand that rebuilding trust takes time, and that you might have hard days or questions. I don’t want to rush you, pressure you to forgive me, or ask you to pretend it never happened just so I can feel better. You’re allowed to feel what you feel. I want to listen without getting defensive, be honest even when it’s uncomfortable, and show through what I do , over time , that I understand the seriousness of the hurt I caused.

Thank you for choosing to give us a chance, even when I gave you reasons not to. I know staying doesn’t mean the hurt disappears, and it doesn’t mean I can assume everything is okay. I’m grateful for you, and I want to treat that chance with the care it deserves. I want us to face what happened together, but I know it’s my responsibility to own my part and change my behaviour.

I still dream about our future , our quiet home, our little Bannana Bob, the life we’ve talked about building. But I know a future like that has to be built on trust, and trust is something I have to earn through my actions every day. I can’t promise I’ll never make a mistake; I can promise to take accountability, tell the truth, respect you, and keep doing the work instead of expecting you to carry the hurt alone.

I love you, Prapthi. I’m sorry for the ways I hurt you and for misusing the trust you placed in me. I don’t take your decision to stay lightly. I want to become better not just because I’m afraid of losing you, but because you deserve a love that is honest, safe, and steady. When things get hard, I hope it can be us facing the problem together , never me against you. Us, with honesty, patience, and real effort.

I’m sorry. I love you. I’ll show you. ♡
